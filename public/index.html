The Difference

Why the processing method matters

Most coconut syrups and coconut nectars on the market are made by mixing coconut sugar and water. In conventional coconut sugar production, coconut sap is typically heated for several hours over high heat (above 120°C) for crystalline sugar, while such saps may also contain additives or chemical preservatives like lime (calcium oxide) or sodium metabisulfite that are added during conventional sap collection to slow fermentation.

Both this prolonged high heating and the chemical preservatives cause caramelization and Maillard reactions deteriorating the physical and chemical properties of the sap, causing significant nutrient loss and altering its natural aroma and taste.

The main difference is that takes a more direct route from fresh coconut blossom sap to finished syrup. We do not make coconut sugar and reconstitute it with water. Our approach combines a patented preservative-free and chemical-free sap collection method, hygienic handling, and carefully controlled low-temp evaporation below 100°C.

What naturally retains

This preserves natural nutrients — simple sugars, electrolytes, fibers, 17 amino acids, zinc, vitamin C and B-vitamins, and many other trace elements — plus the natural coconut blossom aroma and distinctive taste.

More than just sweetness

The result is a functional, multi-nutrient sweet liquid directly made from 100% natural sap, a lower-glycemic alternative to refined sugar and a healthy alternative to honey, maple syrup, and agave. It is USDA Organic Certified (100% organic).
